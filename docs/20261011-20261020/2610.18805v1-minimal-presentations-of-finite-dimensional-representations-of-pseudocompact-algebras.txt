MINIMAL PRESENTATIONS OF FINITE-DIMENSIONAL REPRESENTATIONS OF PSEUDOCOMPACT ALGEBRAS 

CHRISTOF GEISS, CAROLIN HARTUNG, DANIEL LABARDINI-FRAGOSO, AND JAN SCHRÖER 

Abstract. We give a general formula for minimal projective presentations and for minimal discrete-injective presentations of finite-dimensional modules over basic pseudocompact alge-bras. We also show that the representation theoretic g-vector of a module coincides with its DWZ-g-vector. These results generalize the description that Derksen, Weyman and Zelevinsky gave of minimal projective presentations for modules over certain finite-dimensional Jacobian algebras. In particular, we can drop their finiteness assumption and we may work over fields of arbitrary characteristic.
