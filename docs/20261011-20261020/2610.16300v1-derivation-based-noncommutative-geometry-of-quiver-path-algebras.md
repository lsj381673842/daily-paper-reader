## 摘要
本文研究由箭图产生的路径代数的基于导子的非交换几何：通过识别一个导子 Lie 代数，并由此构造微分演算。我们证明该微分演算的性质与箭图结构密切相关。特别地，通过引入分离箭图的概念，我们找到了箭图上的一个条件，该条件等价于微分形式模为投射双模。对于此类箭图，可以构造双模联络，以及作为黎曼度量非交换类似物的双模形式。作为上述概念的示例，我们给出一类箭图，对其可以构造 Levi-Civita 联络，即与双模形式相容的无挠联络。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study derivation based noncommutative geometry of path algebras arising from quivers by identifying a Lie algebra of derivations from which a differential calculus is constructed. We show that properties of the differential calculus are intimately connected with the structure of the quiver. In particular, by introducing the notion of a separating quiver, we find a condition on the quiver which is equivalent to the module of differential forms being a projective bimodule. For such quivers, one can construct bimodule connections, as well as bimodule forms which serve as noncommutative analogues of the Riemannian metric. As an illustration of the above concepts, we present a class of quivers for which one can construct Levi-Civita connections, i.e. torsion free connections compatible with a bimodule form.
