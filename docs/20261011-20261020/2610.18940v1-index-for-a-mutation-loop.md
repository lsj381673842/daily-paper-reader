## 摘要
受 3d-3d 对应启发，我们为箭图的一个变异回路 φ 和整数 g ≥ 0 引入指数 I_g^φ。我们证明在一定条件下其整性 I_g^φ ∈ ℤ，这将 Gang--Kim--Yoon 关于三维流形的整性猜想推广到丛代数中变异回路的更广泛框架。我们的指数还可解释为 (1+1) 维 TQFT 的亏格 g 配分函数。我们给出周期为一的变异回路的显式计算。特别地，在广义 Kronecker 箭图情形下，我们给出一个显式公式，将所有亏格 g 的指数生成函数表示为多项式之比。总之，我们为三维流形几何与丛代数之间的相互作用提供了新的洞见。

## Abstract
Motivated by the 3d-3d correspondence, we introduce an index $\mathcal{I}_g^φ$ for a mutation loop $φ$ of a quiver and an integer $g \geq 0$. We prove its integrality $\mathcal{I}_g^φ\in \mathbb{Z}$ under certain conditions, which extends the integrality conjecture of Gang--Kim--Yoon for $3$-manifolds to the broader framework of mutation loops in cluster algebras. Our index also has an interpretation as the genus-$g$ partition function of a $(1+1)$-dimensional TQFT. We provide explicit computations for mutation loops of period one. In particular, in the case of the generalized Kronecker quivers, we give an explicit formula expressing the generating function of the indices for all genera $g$ as a ratio of polynomials. Altogether, we provide a new insight to the interplay between the geometry of $3$-manifolds and cluster algebra.
