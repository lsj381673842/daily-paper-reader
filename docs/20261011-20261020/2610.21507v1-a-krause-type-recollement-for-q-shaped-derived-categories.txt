2.5 The Q-shaped homotopy category. We first recall the terminology used in this construction. Following [2], an exact category is an additive category equipped with a class of kernel–cokernel pairs satisfying Quillen’s axioms. Such a pair X i

−→ Y p

−→ Z is called a conflation; the morphisms i and p are called an infla-tion and a deflation, respectively. An exact category is Frobenius if it has enough projectives and enough injectives, and these two classes coincide. Their common objects are called projective-injective . The stable category has the same objects as the Frobenius category. Its morphism groups are obtained by taking the quotient modulo morphisms that factor through projective-injective objects.
