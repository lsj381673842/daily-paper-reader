GLUING WEAK APPROXIMABILITY ALONG RECOLLEMENTS 

PAT LANK Abstract. We study whether weak approximability glues along recollements of trian-gulated categories that are compactly generated by a single object. This is applied to derived categories of complexes with quasi-coherent cohomology and prescribed support for quasi-compact quasi-separated algebraic spaces.

Introduction Approximable triangulated categories, and their weak variant, were introduced by Neeman [Nee21a, Nee21b, Nee26]. The theory has been used to settle conjectures of Bondal–Van den Bergh [Nee21b] and of Antieau–Gepner–Heller [Nee24]. It has also found applications to Fourier–Mukai theory for singular schemes [DLM25, GLMP25, GL26]. See [CNS25] for a survey of recent developments. Roughly speaking, a triangulated category with small coproducts and a compact gen-erator G is weakly approximable if every object in the aisle of a suitable t -structure can be suitably approximated from small coproducts of a uniformly bounded range of shifts of G . For example, if X is a quasi-compact quasi-separated scheme and Z ⊆ X is a closed subset with quasi-compact complement, Dqc ,Z (X ) is weakly approximable [Nee24, Theorem 3.2(iv)]. The main result is the following.

Theorem 1.1 is the analog for weak approximability of [BNP23, Theorem 5.1] by Burke– Neeman–Pauwels. Loc. cit. shows that D is approximable if DZ and DU are approximable and Hom (G ′,G ′ [n]) = 0 for n ≫ 0. The ‘only if’ direction of Theorem 1.1 is formal (cf. [BNP23, Remark 3]). Thus, the work lies in the ‘if’ direction. The proof in [BNP23] lifts approximations from DZ to D. This is done by induction on the number of cones needed to build them [BNP23, Lemmas 5.2 to 5.5]. At each step, the bound on the lift is worse than on its target. Hence, the constants grow with the number of cones. Approximability of DZ bounds this number. No such bound need exist if DZ is only weakly approximable. Instead, we lift a single shift of a compact generator so that it lies in the same shifted aisle as its target (e.g. D≤K ). See Lemma 4.6. Consequently, the cone of a lift satisfies the same bound, which gives control on the desired objects. See Proposition 4.8. A closure argument of this type also appears in unpublished work of Pauwels. See Remark 5.8. Here the constants are explicit: if A works for DZ , DU and the vanishing Hom (G , D≤− A) = 0 for the compact generator G of Lemma 4.3(2), then 3 A works for D.We discuss Corollary 1.2. In general for schemes, Dqc ,Z need not be approximable [Nee24, Remark 8.1]. Hence, [BNP23, Theorem 5.1] does not apply, and the weak analog is necessary. The proof of Corollary 1.2 combines Neeman’s result for schemes [Nee24, Theorem 3.2(iv)] with the induction principle for algebraic spaces [Sta26, Tag 09IT].
