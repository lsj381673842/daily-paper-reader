INDEX FOR A MUTATION LOOP 

TSUKASA ISHIBASHI AND YUJI TERASHIMA 

Abstract. Motivated by the 3d-3d correspondence, we introduce an index Iϕg for a mutation loop ϕ of a quiver and an integer g ≥ 0. We prove its integrality Iϕg ∈ Z

under certain conditions, which extends the integrality conjecture of Gang–Kim–Yoon [GKY21] for 3-manifolds to the broader framework of mutation loops in cluster algebras. Our index also has an interpretation as the genus-g partition function of a (1 + 1)-dimensional TQFT. We provide explicit computations for mutation loops of period one. In particular, in the case of the generalized Kronecker quivers, we give an explicit formula expressing the generating function of the indices for all genera g as a ratio of polynomials. Altogether, we provide a new insight to the interplay between the geometry of 3-manifolds and cluster algebra.

In the rich and fruitful interplay between the physics and mathematics of quantum fields, quantum field theory continues to provide new perspectives and conjectures that often appear unexpected from a mathematical point of view. In particular, recent de-velopments have revealed a number of intriguing relationships between quantum quanti-ties arising in quantum field theory and topological invariants in mathematics. Recently, Gang, Kim, and Yoon [GKY21], based on the 3d–3d correspondence [TY11, TY13, DG13, DGG14, CCV11], proposed that, for a three-dimensional gauge theory on Σ g × S1 labeled by a 3-manifold M , the index Ig can be identified with a sum of ( g − 1)-st powers of Rei-demeister torsions associated with M . See also [GKP20, BGP20]. This correspondence provides a bridge between the geometric information of a 3-manifold and the quantum in-formation of the corresponding gauge theory, and offers an important clue for uncovering new mathematical relationships between the two.

Based on this proposal, they formulated a mathematical conjecture asserting that this sum of ( g − 1)-st powers of Reidemeister torsions should be an integer as a consequence of the integrality of the index [GKY21, Conjecture 3.1]. On the other hand, the 3d–3d correspondence was extended in [TY14] to the setting of three-dimensional gauge theories labeled by mutation loops of quivers rather than by 3-manifolds. This extension makes it possible to transfer structures arising from 3-manifolds into the framework of cluster theory [FZ02, FG09]. 1.5. Further perspectives. The definition of the index introduced in this paper is based on a cluster-theoretic analogue of Reidemeister torsion for 3-manifolds. We ex-pect that this analogue will play an important role as a new framework for unifying 3-manifolds and mutation loops in cluster algebras. In particular, it may provide a cluster-theoretic counterpart of projects associating modular tensor categories to 3-manifolds [CGK20, CQW23], the knot–quiver correspondence [KRSS17, KRSS19, EKL20], and the knot–gauge correspondence [MTT21]. More broadly, these connections may reveal new relationships between cluster algebra and gauge theory that are not visible from the ex-isting frameworks. 

Organization of the paper. In Section 2, we recall the basics on mutation loops, their description by mutation networks [TY14, Miz20], and their refinement by Mizuno [Miz21a]. These provide a useful tool to investigate the fixed point locus. In Section 3, we introduce the index of a mutation loop, its refined version, and the generating function with the simplest example of type A2. The integrality of the (refined) index for g ≥

1 is discussed in Section 4. Section 5 provides a (1 + 1)-dimensional interpretation, and a vanishing condition for the genus-0 index. Finally, in Section 6, we give explicit computations for the mutation loops of period one.
