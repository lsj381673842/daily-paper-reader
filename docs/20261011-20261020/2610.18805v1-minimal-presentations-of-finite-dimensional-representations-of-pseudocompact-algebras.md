## 摘要
我们给出了基本伪紧代数上有限维模的极小投射呈现与极小离散内射呈现的一般公式。我们还证明了模的表示论 g-向量与其 DWZ-g-向量一致。这些结果推广了 Derksen、Weyman 和 Zelevinsky 对某些有限维雅可比代数上模的极小投射呈现所给出的描述。特别地，我们可以去掉他们的有限性假设，并且可以在任意特征的域上工作。

## Abstract
We give a general formula for minimal projective presentations and for minimal discrete-injective presentations of finite-dimensional modules over basic pseudocompact algebras. We also show that the representation-theoretic g-vector of a module coincides with its DWZ-g-vector. These results generalize the description that Derksen, Weyman and Zelevinsky gave of minimal projective presentations for modules over certain finite-dimensional Jacobian algebras. In particular, we can drop their finiteness assumption, and we may work over fields of arbitrary characteristic.
