IDEAL QUOTIENTS OF n-EXACT CATEGORIES 

WEIKANG WENG 

Dedicated to Professor Yanan Lin on the occasion of his 70th birthday 

Abstract. In this paper, we extend the notion of a removable subcategory to n-exact categories, and prove that an ideal quotient by a subcategory of projective-injectives inherits an n-exact structure from the original category if and only if the subcategory is removable. In the Frobenius case, the quotient remains Frobenius, and the stable ( n + 2)-angulated categories are equivalent. We give a sufficient condition for a subcategory of projective-injectives to be removable. We also show that the ideal quotient construction is compatible with cluster tilting. Finally, we apply the construction to 2-cluster tilting subcategories of the category of ACM bundles on a Geigle–Lenzing projective plane, obtaining quotients for both natural and relative 2-exact structures. 

1. Introduction 

Exact categories in the sense of Quillen [20] provide a framework for homolog-ical algebra in additive categories equipped with a chosen class of kernel-cokernel pairs; see [1]. The stable category of a Frobenius exact category, obtained by fac-toring out its projective-injective objects, carries a natural triangulated structure [8]. A related question is when an ideal quotient by a subcategory of projective-injective objects inherits the original exact structure. Chen [4, Theorem 3.1] gave sufficient conditions for such a quotient of a Frobenius category to remain Frobe-nius. Demonet–Iyama [6, Theorem 3.6] showed that the quotient inherits the exact structure precisely when the quotient functor sends inflations and deflations to categorical monomorphisms and epimorphisms, respectively. Jasso [13] introduced n-exact categories by formulating higher analogues of the axioms of exact categories, using n-exact sequences in place of short exact se-quences. For n = 1, these recover the usual exact categories. The stable cate-gory of a Frobenius n-exact category carries a natural ( n + 2)-angulated structure in the sense of Geiss–Keller–Oppermann [7]. Herschend–Liu–Nakaoka [10] intro-duced n-exangulated categories as higher analogues of extriangulated categories of Nakaoka–Palu [19]. They encompass both n-exact and ( n+2)-angulated categories. Hu–Zhang–Zhou [11, Theorem 3.1] established a criterion for an ideal quotient to inherit an n-exangulated structure. However, the resulting n-exangulated structure need not be n-exact, even when the original category is n-exact [11, Example 3.4]. In this paper, we give a direct treatment of ideal quotients of n-exact categories, including the Frobenius case, and establish related results. In particular, we apply the ideal quotient construction to Geigle–Lenzing projective planes.
