## 摘要
我们研究弱逼近性是否沿由单个对象紧生成的三角范畴的recollement粘合。该结果应用于拟紧拟分离代数空间上具有拟凝聚上同调和指定支集的复形的导出范畴。

## Abstract
We study whether weak approximability glues along recollements of triangulated categories that are compactly generated by a single object. This is applied to derived categories of complexes with quasi-coherent cohomology and prescribed support for quasi-compact quasi-separated algebraic spaces.
