## 摘要
我们将具有斜对称交换矩阵的无系数无圈仿射簇代数中正则模的乘法公式与 Reading-Stella 的虚交换关系联系起来。在 Zhou-Zhu 的构造中引入参数 $z$，我们定义了簇管上的加权簇特征，它满足 $C_{n-1}$ 型广义交换关系，并在不可分解刚性对象与簇变量之间给出双射。利用 Ding-Xiao-Xu 的乘法公式与差分性质，我们证明非齐次管嵌入在 $z\mapsto X_δ$ 下诱导到普通仿射簇代数的单同态。与 Reading-Stella 关系的比较将 $X_δ$ 等同于相应的 theta 函数，从而给出广义交换参数的表示论解释。

## Abstract
We relate multiplication formulas for regular modules in coefficient-free acyclic affine cluster algebras with skew-symmetric exchange matrices to the imaginary exchange relations of Reading-Stella. Introducing a parameter $z$ into the construction of Zhou-Zhu, we define a weighted cluster character on cluster tubes that satisfies generalized exchange relations of type $C_{n-1}$ and yields a bijection between indecomposable rigid objects and cluster variables. Using the multiplication formulas and difference property of Ding-Xiao-Xu, we show that non-homogeneous tube embeddings induce injective homomorphisms into ordinary affine cluster algebras under $z\mapsto X_δ$. Comparison with Reading-Stella's relations identifies $X_δ$ with the corresponding theta function, giving a representation-theoretic interpretation of the generalized exchange parameter.
