## 摘要
作为将 Artin-Tate-Van den Bergh 的齐次坐标环技巧推广到扭分次 Calabi-Yau (tCY) 代数这一计划的一部分，我们启动了非连通分次代数点模的研究。我们定义了路径代数分次商的弱点模与强点模，并计算了它们各自的模叠。我们描述了这些点叠与连通分次情形中经典点概形之间的关系，并分析了若干 2 维和 3 维 tCY 代数的点叠。

## Abstract
As part of a program to extend the homogeneous coordinate ring techniques of Artin-Tate-Van den Bergh to twisted graded Calabi-Yau (tCY) algebras, we initiate the study of point modules of non-connected graded algebras. We define weak and strong point modules of graded quotients of path algebras and compute their respective moduli stacks. We describe the relation between these point stacks and the classical point schemes in the connected graded setting, and we analyze the point stacks of several 2 and 3-dimensional tCY algebras.
