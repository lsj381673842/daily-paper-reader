## 摘要
$\mathbb{P}$-对象由 Huybrechts 和 Thomas 引入，作为球状对象的推广。我们分类了由 Krug 意义下的 $\mathbb{P}^n[k]$-对象生成的三角范畴上的权结构与 t-结构。对于 $n=1$，这恢复了由 Holm、Jorgensen 和 Yang 给出的由球状对象生成的三角范畴上的权结构与 t-结构的分类。我们还计算了由 $\mathbb{P}^n[k]$-对象生成的三角范畴的 Koszul 对偶模型。

## Abstract
$\mathbb{P}$-objects were introduced by Huybrechts and Thomas as a generalization of spherical objects. We classify the weight structures and t-structures on the triangulated category generated by a $\mathbb{P}^n[k]$-object in the sense of Krug. For $n=1$, this recovers the classification of weight structures and t-structures on the triangulated category generated by a spherical object due to Holm, Jorgensen and Yang. We also compute the Koszul-dual model of the triangulated category generated by a $\mathbb{P}^n[k]$-object.
