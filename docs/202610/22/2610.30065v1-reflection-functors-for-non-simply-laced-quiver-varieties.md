## 摘要
我们证明了 Nakajima 箭图簇的非单边型类似物上的反射函子之间的辫关系；这些类似物定义为某些带势箭图的临界轨迹。我们将该框架推广到矩阵分解范畴和临界 $K$-理论。

## Abstract
We prove the braid relations between reflection functors on non-simply-laced analogues of Nakajima quiver varieties, which are defined as critical loci of certain quivers with potential. We extend this framework to categories of matrix factorizations and critical $K$-theory.
