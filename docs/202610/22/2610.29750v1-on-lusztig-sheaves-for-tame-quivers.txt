ON LUSZTIG SHEAVES FOR TAME QUIVERS 

YUMENG WU, JIE XIAO 

Abstract. Let Q be a connected quiver of tame type, and let U − 

> v

be the positive part of the corresponding quantized affine algebra. In Lusztig’s construction of the canonical basis of U − 

> v

,the simple perverse sheaves occurring, up to shift, as direct summands of the flag sheaf complexes are given by intersection cohomology complexes IC( X(c, m ), Lχ). We study the restrictions of these intersection cohomology complexes to the locally closed subsets Y (c, m ) obtained by fixing the preprojective, non-homogeneous regular, and preinjective summands in the category rep Q. We prove pointwise purity on the loci where the homogeneous regular part is a direct sum of pairwise non-isomorphic quasi-simple modules, and establish a semisimple decomposition of the restrictions to the corresponding larger strata. The graded multiplicities of the local systems associated with irreducible characters of sym-metric groups determine the transition coefficients from the canonical basis to the PBW basis constructed via quiver representations. This gives a geometric explanation of the positivity of these coefficients and the unitriangularity of the transition matrix. 

Introduction 

Ringel [27] realized the negative part U − 

> v

of a quantum group using Hall algebras of Dynkin quiv-ers, and Green [14] extended this realization to arbitrary finite acyclic quivers by identifying U −

> v

with the corresponding generic twisted composition algebra. For quivers of Dynkin type, the orbits in the representation varieties correspond to elements of a Poincar´ e–Birkhoff–Witt (PBW) basis. Lusztig [18, 19] constructed the canonical basis by algebraic and geometric methods. In the geo-metric construction, its elements are represented by simple perverse sheaves on the representation varieties. For a connected quiver Q of tame type, Lusztig [20] used the representation theory of Q to describe the simple perverse sheaves that realize the canonical basis of the corresponding quantum affine algebra. These sheaves have the form IC( X(c, m ), Lχ), with support X(c, m ), where the local systems Lχ are associated with irreducible characters of symmetric groups. Further details, including the description for arbitrary orientations, can be found in [16]. For quantum affine algebras, canonical bases can also be described through algebraic construc-tions of PBW bases; see [3, 4, 25, 30]. Constructions using representations of tame quivers were developed in [17, 34], with the cyclic-quiver results in [10] as an ingredient. In particular, Xiao, Xu, and Zhao [34] proved that the bar-invariant basis obtained from their PBW basis coincides with Lusztig’s canonical basis and identified the corresponding indices. Lusztig’s work [18] and Kato’s work [15] show how a categorical description of PBW bases can explain the positivity and unitriangularity of the transition matrix from the canonical basis to a PBW basis. We seek such a description using constructible complexes on quiver representation varieties and their relation to Lusztig sheaves. In this paper, we establish this relation for connected quivers of tame type by studying restrictions of Lusztig sheaves and their Frobenius weights. We describe the results more precisely. The required tube filtrations mix prescribed semisimple regular quotients with unrestricted quotients of dimension δ; their pointwise purity is established in Lemma 5.2. In Section 3, we prove the restriction theorem. In Section 4, we derive the decomposition and transition formulas from pointwise purity, whose proof occupies Section 5.
