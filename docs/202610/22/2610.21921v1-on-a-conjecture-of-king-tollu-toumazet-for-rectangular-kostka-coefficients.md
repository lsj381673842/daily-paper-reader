## 摘要
King、Tollu 和 Toumazet 的一个猜想预测了小 Littlewood--Richardson 系数在伸缩下的行为：若 $c^ν_{λ,μ}=3$，则对每个正整数 $M$，$c^{Mν}_{Mλ,Mμ}$ 等于 $2M+1$ 或 $\binom{M+2}{2}$。这对应于二次曲线或射影平面的 Hilbert 函数。我们对所有矩形 Kostka 系数 $K_{λ,\mathbf{a}}$ 证明他们的猜想。我们将其实现为 Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}(k,n)$ 的齐次坐标环的权重数，或等价地，实现为维数向量 $β=(k,1,1,\dotsc,1)$ 的 $n$-子空间箭图的半不变量的权空间。对于权 $σ$，我们证明：若 $β$ 是 $σ$-稳定的，则当 $k=2$ 时相应权空间的维数至少为 $n-2$，且对于除 $\mathrm{Gr}(2,4)$、$\mathrm{Gr}(2,5)$ 和 $\mathrm{Gr}(3,5)$ 之外的每个 Grassmannian，维数至少为 $4$。这三种情形恰好是相应的 $σ$-稳定表示的模空间维数至多为二的情形。通过 $σ$-稳定分解，两种可能值确定如下：对 $\mathrm{Gr}(2,4)$ 为 $2M+1$，对 $\mathrm{Gr}(2,5)$ 和 $\mathrm{Gr}(3,5)$ 为 $\binom{M+2}{2}$。

## Abstract
A conjecture of King, Tollu and Toumazet predicts the behavior of small Littlewood--Richardson coefficients under stretching: if $c^ν_{λ,μ}=3$, then $c^{Mν}_{Mλ,Mμ}$ equals $2M+1$ or $\binom{M+2}{2}$ for every positive integers $M$. This corresponds to the Hilbert functions of a conic, or of the projective plane. We prove their conjecture for all rectangular Kostka coefficients $K_{λ,\mathbf{a}}$. We realize them as the weight multiplicities of the homogeneous coordinate ring of the Grassmannian $\mathrm{Gr}(k,n)$, or equivalently the weight spaces of semi-invariants of the $n$-subspace quiver with dimension vector $β=(k,1,1,\dotsc,1)$. For a weight $σ$, we show that if $β$ is $σ$-stable, then the dimension of the corresponding weight space is at least $n-2$ when $k=2$ and at least $4$ for every Grassmannian other than $\mathrm{Gr}(2,4)$, $\mathrm{Gr}(2,5)$ and $\mathrm{Gr}(3,5)$. These three cases are precisely when the associated moduli space of $σ$-stable representations has dimension at most two. Via $σ$-stable decomposition, the two alternatives are determined as follows: $2M+1$ for $\mathrm{Gr}(2,4)$, and $\binom{M+2}{2}$ for $\mathrm{Gr}(2,5)$ and $\mathrm{Gr}(3,5)$.
