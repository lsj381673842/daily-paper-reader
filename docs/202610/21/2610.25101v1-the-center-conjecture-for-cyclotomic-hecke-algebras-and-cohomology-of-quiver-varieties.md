## 摘要
对于无环边的箭图，我们构造了从以任意域为系数的 Nakajima 箭图簇的上同调到该域上相应分圆 KLR 代数的 Jucys--Murphy 中心的一个同构。在仿射型 A^{(1)}_{e-1} 中，将此与维数计算相结合可表明：该 Jucys--Murphy 中心在特征 0、特征不整除 e 以及特征等于 e 时与完整中心重合。这可由一个几何定理推出，该定理表明这些上同调环的直和作为环路代数的一个整形式上的模是余循环的。作为推论，我们证明：对于任意域上 q≠1 的 G(r,1,n) 型分圆 Hecke 代数，其中心由其 Jucys--Murphy 元素中的对称多项式生成；并且在任何使 q-1 可逆的交换整环上同样成立。我们还证明了 Krylov、Paegelow 和 Shlykov 猜想在整情形下的推广，该猜想将 Gieseker 簇的核的等变 K-理论与一般分圆 Hecke 代数的余中心等同起来，并包含该猜想到正特征的自然推广。

## Abstract
For a quiver without edge loops, we construct an isomorphism from the cohomology of a Nakajima quiver variety with coefficients in an arbitrary field to the Jucys--Murphy center of the corresponding cyclotomic KLR algebra over that field. In affine type $A^{(1)}_{e-1}$, combining this with a dimension count shows that this Jucys--Murphy center coincides with the full center in characteristic $0$, when the characteristic does not divide $e$, and when the characteristic equals $e$. This follows from a geometric theorem showing that the sum of these cohomology rings is cocyclic as a module over an integral form of a loop algebra.   As a consequence, we prove that the center of a cyclotomic Hecke algebra of type $G(r,1,n)$ with $q\neq 1$ over any field is generated by symmetric polynomials in its Jucys--Murphy elements, and the same holds over any commutative domain in which $q-1$ is invertible. We also prove a generalization to the integral case of the conjecture of Krylov, Paegelow and Shlykov identifying the equivariant $K$-theory of the core of a Gieseker variety with the cocenter of the generic cyclotomic Hecke algebra, including the natural extension of this conjecture to positive characteristic.
