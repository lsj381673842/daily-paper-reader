## 摘要
我们研究 Taft 范畴的 $2$-表示理论，该范畴是 Taft 代数上有限维模构成的张量范畴。该范畴有两个左胞腔。我们首先在其带基表示环上对合适的传递模进行分类。对于上胞腔，我们回顾 Etingof 和 Ostrik 的一个构造，该构造给出了一族无限多个两两不等价的单 $2$-表示。对于下胞腔，我们转向 Taft 范畴的半单化，并将一个特殊的融合子范畴等同于伴随 $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ Verlinde 范畴。利用与 $\mathsf{ADET}$-图相关联的代数对象和范畴 Clifford 理论，我们对这个半单化的正则精确不可分解模范畴进行分类。对于这些正则模范畴，分裂 Grothendieck 群是一个完全不变量。最后，我们确定 Taft 范畴的两个胞腔 $2$-表示。

## Abstract
We study the $2$-representation theory of the Taft category, the tensor category of finite-dimensional modules over the Taft algebra. This category has two left cells. We first classify suitable transitive modules over its based representation ring. For the upper cell, we recall a construction by Etingof and Ostrik giving an infinite family of pairwise nonequivalent simple $2$-representations. For the bottom cell, we pass to the semisimplification of the Taft category and identify a distinguished fusion subcategory with an adjoint $\mathfrak{sl}_2$ Verlinde category. Using algebra objects associated with $\mathsf{ADET}$-diagrams and categorical Clifford theory, we classify the regular exact indecomposable module categories of this semisimplification. For these regular module categories, the split Grothendieck group is a complete invariant. Finally, we identify the two cell $2$-representations of the Taft category.
