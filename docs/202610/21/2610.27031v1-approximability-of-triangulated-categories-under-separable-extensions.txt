Abstract 

In this paper, we investigate the behavior of approximability under separable extensions of triangulated categories. Let B be an approximable triangulated category and let T be a smashing, stably separable exact monad on B. We prove that if T is right t-exact with respect to a t-structure in the preferred equivalence class, then the category of T-modules T-Mod B is again an approximable triangulated category. As an application, we show that, under suitable assumptions, a smashing localization functor on an approximable triangulated category induces a recollement of approximable triangulated categories, thereby yielding a converse-type result to the gluing theorem of Burke–Neeman–Pauwels. Finally, we prove that separable extensions restrict naturally to the intrinsic subcategories associated with approximable triangulated categories. 

# 1 Introduction 

Approximable triangulated categories were introduced by Neeman [24, 26] as a powerful framework to study the triangulated categories arising in algebraic geometry and topology. Roughly speaking, a triangulated category with coproducts is approximable if it is compactly generated by a single object C, such that any “bounded above” object can be systematically approximated by objects finitely built from coproducts of shifts of C. This class encompasses the derived categories of rings and quasi-compact, separated schemes, as well as the homotopy category of spectra. The theory has proved useful in extending the foundational results of Bondal and Van den Bergh [9] and Rouquier [27]; see [25, 26]. The stability of approximability under standard categorical operations has been investigated in several directions. Notably, Burke, Neeman, and Pauwels [12] demonstrated that approximability be-haves well under gluing techniques arising from recollements. Nevertheless, approximability remains a delicate property. Even algebraic triangulated categories possessing a single compact generator, such as the derived category of an arbitrary dg-algebra, can fail to be approximable unless their homology groups vanish for positive degrees [26, Remark 5.3]. Parallel to recollements, separable extensions provide another fundamental operation for decom-posing and constructing triangulated categories. Introduced by Balmer [1], the theory of separable extensions of triangulated categories has become an important tool in tensor-triangular geometry and has subsequently found applications in modular representation theory [2, 5] and algebraic geometry [3]. It encompasses several important constructions, including Bousfield localizations [1, Example 6.3],

T is an additive monad . When the category B is equipped with a suspension Σ, an additive monad T

is said to be stable if T , θ, and μ commute with the suspension: T Σ = Σ T , θΣ = Σ θ, and μΣ = Σ μ.If moreover, A and B are suspended and F and G commute with the suspension, we assume that the adjunction respects the suspension, meaning that θ and ε commute with the suspension. Theorem 2.8. Let B be a triangulated category and T = ( T, θ, μ ) a stably separable exact monad on 

B. Then the Eilenberg–Moore category T-Mod B admits a unique canonical pre-triangulated structure such that F T and GT are both exact functors. Remark 2.9. (1) It is well known that if we assume B admits a triangulation of order N ≥ 3, then T-Mod B is indeed a valid triangulated category [1, Section 5]. In the remainder of this paper, we shall simply treat T-Mod B as a triangulated category equipped with this canonical triangulated structure. (2) Let T = ( T, θ, μ ) be a stable monad on B. By [13, Lemma 3.2], T is separable if and only if 

GT is a separable functor, where a right adjoint functor GT is separable if and only if its counit (viewed as a natural transformation) admitting a section; see, for example, [13, Lemma 2.2]. (3) Conversely, given an adjunction ( F, G ; θ, ε ) from B to A with A idempotent complete, G is separable if and only if the forgetful functor GT : T-Mod B → B is separable and the comparison functor φ : A → T-Mod B is an equivalence [13, Corollary 3.6] (i.e., G is monadic). Based on the descriptions above, we make the following hypothesis for the remainder of this paper. 

Hypothesis 2.10. Let B be a triangulated category and let T = ( T, θ, μ ) be a stably separable exact monad on B. Assume that T-Mod B admits a canonical triangulated structure. If B admits coproducts, assume moreover that T is smashing , meaning that T preserves coproducts.
