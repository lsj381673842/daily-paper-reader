## 摘要
本文研究三角范畴的可分离扩张下可逼近性的行为。设 $\mathcal{B}$ 是一个可逼近三角范畴，且 $\mathbf{T}$ 是 $\mathcal{B}$ 上的一个 smashing、稳定可分离的正合单子。我们证明：若 $\mathbf{T}$ 关于某个属于优选等价类的 $t$-结构是右 $t$-正合的，则 $\mathbf{T}$-模范畴 $\mathbf{T}\text{-Mod}_{\mathcal{B}}$ 仍是一个可逼近三角范畴。作为应用，我们证明：在适当假设下，可逼近三角范畴上的一个 smashing 局部化函子会诱导可逼近三角范畴的一个 recollement，从而给出 Burke--Neeman--Pauwels 黏合定理的一个逆型结果。最后，我们证明可分离扩张自然地限制到与可逼近三角范畴相关联的内蕴子范畴上。

## Abstract
In this paper, we investigate the behavior of approximability under separable extensions of triangulated categories. Let $\mathcal{B}$ be an approximable triangulated category and let $\mathbf{T}$ be a smashing, stably separable exact monad on $\mathcal{B}$. We prove that if $\mathbf{T}$ is right $t$-exact with respect to a $t$-structure in the preferred equivalence class, then the category of $\mathbf{T}$-modules $\mathbf{T}\text{-Mod}_{\mathcal{B}}$ is again an approximable triangulated category. As an application, we show that, under suitable assumptions, a smashing localization functor on an approximable triangulated category induces a recollement of approximable triangulated categories, thereby yielding a converse-type result to the gluing theorem of Burke--Neeman--Pauwels. Finally, we prove that separable extensions restrict naturally to the intrinsic subcategories associated with approximable triangulated categories.
