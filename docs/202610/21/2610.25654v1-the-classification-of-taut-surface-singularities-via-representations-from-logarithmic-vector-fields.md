## 摘要
我们通过有限维表示研究例外圈上的对数向量场，并重新得到 Laufer 关于 taut 正规复曲面奇点的分类。对于具有两个节点的图，限制映射定义了固定 quiver 的表示，其维数独立于分量个数及其自交数而有界。我们将限制上同调等同于一个扩张群，并利用区间分解将其消没性归结为有理链上的插值问题。这些计算确定了分类条件，而在每个解析实现上的消没性证明了充分性，包括非有理情形。障碍圈还给出统一判据：一个图是 taut 的当且仅当对每个解析实现都有 $H^1(5E,S_X\|_{5E})=0$，其中 $S_X$ 是环境对数切丛。我们证明 5 是使该判据对所有图都成立的最小正整数。

## Abstract
We study logarithmic vector fields on exceptional cycles through finite-dimensional representations and recover Laufer's classification of taut normal complex surface singularities. For graphs with two nodes, restriction maps define representations of a fixed quiver, with dimensions bounded independently of the number of components and their self-intersections. We identify restriction cohomology with an extension group and use interval decomposition to reduce its vanishing to interpolation on a rational chain. These calculations determine the classification conditions, and vanishing on every analytic realization proves sufficiency, including the nonrational cases. The obstruction cycles also give a uniform criterion: a graph is taut if and only if $H^1(5E,S_X|_{5E})=0$ for every analytic realization, where $S_X$ is the ambient logarithmic tangent bundle. We prove that five is the smallest positive integer for which this criterion holds for all graphs.
