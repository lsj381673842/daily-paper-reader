## 摘要
本文中，我们为 dg 模范畴与弯曲 dg 余模范畴构造了从代数 K-理论到 Hochschild 同调的显式 Dennis 迹映射，并证明其与导出 Koszul 对偶的相容性。为此，我们构造了一个自然链映射 τ，从新定义的加厚神经上的链复形到 dg 循环神经的总复形，其显式公式包含态射与相干高阶同伦，推广了 McCarthy 的方法。该 τ 构造既适用于 dg 代数上的模，也适用于有余单位且余幂零的弯曲 dg 余代数上的 cdg 余模。将 τ 与 dg 富化的 Waldhausen S_•-构造以及一个 Hochschild 去循环定理相结合，得到 Dennis 迹映射。对于在导出 Koszul 对偶下相对应的厚子范畴，我们证明诱导的 K-理论等价与 Hochschild 同调同构与这些迹映射构成自然的交换图表。

## Abstract
In this paper, we construct explicit Dennis trace maps from algebraic $K$-theory to Hochschild homology for dg module and curved dg comodule categories, and prove their compatibility with derived Koszul duality. For this, we construct a natural chain map $τ$ from the chains on the newly defined thickened nerve to the total complex of the dg cyclic nerve, with explicit formulas incorporating morphisms and coherent higher homotopies, which generalizes McCarthy's approach. This $τ$ construction works both for modules over dg algebras and for cdg comodules over counital conilpotent curved dg coalgebras. Combining the $τ$ with the dg-enriched Waldhausen $S_\bullet$-construction and a Hochschild delooping theorem yields the Dennis trace maps. For thick subcategories corresponding under derived Koszul duality, we prove that the induced $K$-theory equivalences and Hochschild homology isomorphisms fit into natural commutative diagrams with these trace maps.
