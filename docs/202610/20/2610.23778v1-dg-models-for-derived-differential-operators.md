## 摘要
我们证明，对于任何在域 $k$ 上本质有限型的代数 $R$，Smith–Van den Bergh 的导出函子允许一个 dg 增强。特别地，对任意 $R$-模 $M$，可以联系一个微分算子的 dg 代数 $E_M$，其上同调是 $M$ 上 $k$-线性微分算子（经典）环的一个幂零加厚。我们证明这些 dg 代数纳入 Dwyer–Greenlees 的 Koszul 对偶框架，并且当 $M=R$ 且 $k=\mathbb{C}$ 时特化为 Yang 研究过的导出微分算子环。特别地，在固定一个表示 $R=S/I$（其中 $S$ 本质光滑）之后，我们证明一个导出的 Kashiwara 等价，即只要 $\mathrm{supp}_S(M)=\mathrm{V}(I)$，则在 dg $E_M$-模的导出范畴与 $S$ 上集合论意义下支撑在 $I$ 上的 $D$-模的导出范畴之间存在三角范畴等价。最后，在正特征情形，我们证明 dg 代数 $E_M$ 允许用 Frobenius 自同态给出描述，类似于微分算子的经典描述，并且证明使用单纯技术来获得交换代数中一个具有独立意义的结果。

## Abstract
We show that for any algebra $R$ that is essentially of finite type over a field $k$, the derived functors of Smith-Van den Bergh admit a dg enhancement. In particular, for any $R$-module $M$ one can associate a dg algebra of differential operators $E_M$ whose cohomology is a nilpotent thickening of the (classical) ring of $k$-linear differential operators on $M$. We show that these dg algebras fit into a framework of Koszul duality due to Dwyer-Greenlees and specializes to a derived ring of differential operators studied by Yang when $M=R$ and $k=\mathbb{C}$. In particular, upon fixing a presentation $R=S/I$ with $S$ essentially smooth, we show a derived Kashiwara's equivalence, namely that whenever $\mathrm{supp}_S(M)=\mathrm{V}(I)$ then there is an equivalence of triangulated categories between the derived category of dg ${E}_M$-modules and the derived category of $D$-modules on $S$ that are set-theoretically supported on $I$. Finally, in the case of positive characteristic, we show that the dg algebra ${E}_M$ admits a description in terms of the Frobenius endomorphism akin to the classical description of differential operators, and the proof employs simplicial techniques to obtain a result of independent interest in commutative algebra.
