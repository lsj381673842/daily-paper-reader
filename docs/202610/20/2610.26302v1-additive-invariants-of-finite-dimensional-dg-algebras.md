## 摘要
Orlov 已经证明，光滑有限维 DG 代数的 K-理论与 Hochschild 同调等加性不变量，其行为与有限维代数的相应不变量类似。但这种相似性仅限于一定程度：存在有限维 DG 代数的例子，其 Grothendieck 群为零或不可数。我们证明，对于带关系的 DG 箭图，其 Grothendieck 群是自由群且具有预期秩；并且在光滑情形下，所有加性不变量都由基域决定。我们利用这些结果排除某些有限维 DG 代数成为 phantoms。

## Abstract
Orlov has shown that additive invariants such as K-theory and Hochschild homology of smooth finite-dimensional DG-algebras behave similarly to those of finite-dimensional algebras. The similarities only go so far: there are examples of finite-dimensional DG-algebras with vanishing and uncountable Grothendieck groups. We show that for DG-quivers with relations, the Grothendieck groups are free of the expected rank and that, in the smooth case, all additive invariants are determined by the base field. We use our results to rule out certain finite-dimensional DG-algebras from being phantoms.
