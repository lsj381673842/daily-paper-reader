## 摘要
对每个非零交换环 $R$，我们构造一个 $R$ 上的非拓扑三角范畴，其有限生成的厚子范畴是代数的，而 $R$ 的标量作用是忠实的。

## Abstract
Over every nonzero commutative ring $R$, we construct a nontopological triangulated category over $R$, whose finitely generated thick subcategories are alegbraic, while the scalar action of $R$ is faithful.
