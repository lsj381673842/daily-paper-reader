## 摘要
本文研究具有无限多个单对象同构类的张量范畴之间的幺半导出等价。我们引入单移位幺半导出等价的概念，并证明单移位幺半导出等价可导出张量等价。此外，我们研究哪些张量范畴具有如下性质：它们之间的每个幺半导出等价都必然是单移位的，并给出该性质的一些充分条件。作为推论，我们证明 pointed 张量范畴之间的幺半导出等价蕴涵它们之间的张量等价。

## Abstract
In this paper, we investigate monoidal derived equivalences between tensor categories with infinitely many isomorphism classes of simple objects. We introduce the notion of a simple-shifted monoidal derived equivalence and prove that a simple-shifted monoidal derived equivalence gives rise to a tensor equivalence. In addition, we investigate which tensor categories have the property that every monoidal derived equivalence between them is necessarily simple-shifted, and we establish some sufficient conditions for this property. As a corollary, we show that a monoidal derived equivalence between pointed tensor categories implies a tensor equivalence between them.
